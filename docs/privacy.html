Privacy

Quantica Labs Ltd is the controller for Charioteer Sober/Health Coach. This page is plain language for the product. It is not a solicitor’s privacy notice. Have it reviewed before a public launch.

What stays on your account

Your check-in notes, mood, sleep, and craving scores stay on your account. Raw health readings, health flags, height, and Thumos chats stay on your account. A photo stays on your account until you choose to share that photo.

Other people cannot open those private tables. The app does not add a public read policy to them.

What other community members can see

Community is off until you turn it on, and only if you have confirmed you are 18 or over. While it is off, your profile is hidden.

When Community is on, other signed-in adults can see your name and your interests. Everything else starts off. You choose each item with Share on profile:

  • Town or area
  • Stage, such as stopped or cutting down
  • Days sober and milestones
  • The reason lines you pick
  • Money saved and units avoided, as totals only
  • A check-in mood streak, as a count only
  • Health headlines you pick, such as a steps average or a sleep average
  • A progress photo you mark for the community
  • A gratitude line you pick
  • A gratitude photo you pick
  • A vision board caption you pick
  • A Stoic quote you have pinned, if you switch that on

Turning a switch off hides that item on the next view. The People screen shows a preview of what is shared and a list of what is hidden.

A profile picture and the profile gallery are shown to signed-in community members while Community is on. There is no separate switch for each gallery photo, and there is no limit on how many you can add. The profile front shows the first photos, in the order you set. See all photos opens the rest. Removing a photo in Edit profile takes it off the public profile on the next view. The file is a JPEG in the private profile-photos bucket. Location data is stripped before upload. A signed link is used to show it. There is no public file address.

Sensitive information

Days sober, your stage, reasons, money and unit totals, a mood streak, and health headlines are special category data under UK GDPR. The first time you share any of that, the app asks you to confirm. It tells you that other adults in the Charioteer community can see the items you switch on, and that Quantica Labs Ltd is the controller.

Town, a gratitude line, a vision caption, and a pinned Stoic quote are not treated as that same confirmation. A pinned quote is a public-domain line you chose. A progress photo already asks before it is shown to the community.

How the shared view is built

Your choices are stored in profile sharing settings, which only you can read and write. Other signed-in people do not read that table, and they do not read your private rows. They call the public profile function, which returns only the fields you have switched on. It returns nothing if you are signed out, if Community is off, if age is not confirmed, or if either of you has blocked the other.

Gratitude and vision picture files stay in your private storage. A shared gratitude photo is identified on the profile. A shared vision item shows the caption and life area. A progress photo you have marked for the community can be opened, as it could before.

Reports

A report can be sent about a shared profile item. The report goes to the moderation queue.

Your other choices

You can export your data or delete the account. Deleting the account removes the sharing settings with the rest of the account. You can change Community, health, and coach consent at any time.

Apple Health

On iPhone, Charioteer can read Apple Health when you allow it. It reads steps, sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, cardio fitness (VO2 max), walking heart rate, heart rate recovery, workouts (type, duration, distance, energy, and average heart rate), walking, cycling and swimming distance, weight, height, body fat, lean body mass, active energy, and exercise minutes. It does not read blood oxygen. It never writes workouts, heart rate, steps, sleep, or weight. It writes only the meals and water you choose to save.

Readings stay on your account as daily totals: minutes split into strength, cardio, and other, plus the latest cardio fitness, walking heart rate, heart rate recovery, and session heart rate for a day. A raw heart-rate trace from a workout is not stored. Other people cannot see these readings. They are not used for advertising or marketing.

If you turn on the coach as well as health data, a short summary is sent to the model to write the Health Plan and Thumos replies. That summary is the fitness level, the trend in a sentence, weekly minutes, the mix of sessions, and recovery. The raw samples are not sent for that. You can turn the coach off, and you can turn Apple Health off in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Charioteer. The fitness card is an estimate, not a medical assessment.

Activity tracking

Approved by Ben on 26 October 2026 for build 74. Not shipped. This notice is not published ahead of the build. The sentence in the Location section is the one in this build.

Charioteer uses your location while you are on a walk, run, or cycle you start, to track distance and give you updates, including when the screen is locked or you are using another app. You tap Start before measuring begins. An explanation in the app comes before the system permission. The permission stays while using the app. Always location is not added. iOS can show a blue location mark while measuring is on. Measuring stops when you tap Stop, when you discard the outing, when the app is closed, and after the time limit. That limit is six hours. Ben confirmed that length on 26 October 2026. The route of that outing stays in memory on your phone for the outing only. It is cleared when you stop or discard it. If the app is closed mid-outing, the route is lost. The outing path is not sent, including to the account, the route service, the coach, a model, or HeiGIT. It is not written to a log. It is not combined with sobriety logs or included in a data export. A finished activity you choose to keep stores the distance, time, and pace on this phone, not the path.

Spoken lines are on-device text to speech from a fixed list, and only when you pick spoken updates. The phone fills in the time and the pace. There is no microphone and no recording. On screen only does not start an audio session. There is no silent keepalive audio. App Store submission is blocked until a real phone shows music ducks, then returns to full volume after each line, with no leftover audio session, including a phone call and Bluetooth headphones.

Heart rate and active energy are read from Apple Health while the session is in front. The read is foreground only. Background delivery is not turned on. The number stays on this phone and is not sent. It is not exported and it is not written next to sobriety fields. The read stops on Stop, on discard, and at the time limit. Whether UK GDPR Article 9 applies is for a solicitor to confirm.

A gym session on Workouts shows a clock, a heart-rate row, and active energy when Apple Health has it. It does not use location. It does not count reps.

Walk this off (the craving walk) is parked and is not shown. It is not a live feature.

Route finding stays off until you turn it on. Not now leaves the rest of Outside is Good working. The choice stays on this phone. When it is on, the start point, the destination, and any points you add on the map are sent to OpenRouteService, run by HeiGIT and built on OpenStreetMap data, to make the route. A route sends the start, the destination, any points you add on the map, and the travel type. It does not send your name or account number. The outing path is not sent. Route adjusting was approved by Ben on 26 October 2026, in build 74, not shipped. A route sends the coordinates and the travel type only. It does not send your name or account number. HeiGIT sees our server's address, not your phone's. A place search sends the words you type. HeiGIT's transparency statement (https://account.heigit.org/info/transparency-statement) says the full request and response, the IP address, and the API key are kept for 7 days to look for abuse, then deleted. For a route, coordinates rounded to about 1 km, and the request metadata, are kept with no end date. This is done on servers in the EU. The statement is voluntary and not a binding promise. A place search is geocoding. The same statement says the typed place is not kept past those 7 days. Nothing is shared with other people. The map credits OpenStreetMap contributors. Turning this on is not a request to accept OpenStreetMap terms.

The map opens light, on the OpenFreeMap Liberty style. You can switch that map to dark. The choice stays on this phone. Both styles come from tiles.openfreemap.org. In plain words, the map downloads tiles for the area shown from OpenFreeMap, which can reveal the approximate area, not the path or your identity. OpenFreeMap's privacy policy (https://openfreemap.org/privacy/, last updated 8 October 2026, read 26 October 2026) says access logs do not store IP addresses by default, and that error logs which can include an IP address and the requested URL are deleted after 7 days. They may use Cloudflare. A full screen map is the same map, larger. It does not add a permission or another host. The outing path is not sent to OpenFreeMap.

The page still opens if you say no. There is no Always location. A walk, run, or cycle you start can keep location on until you stop, discard it, close the app, or reach the time limit. A saved route stays on your account until you delete it. A live fix is not stored. The outing path is not stored. Live routes can't be shared with anyone. That's to protect your safety. No live-tracking route is sent to HeiGIT.
